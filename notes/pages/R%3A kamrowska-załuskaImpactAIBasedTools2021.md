- [[Abstract]]
	- This paper reviews the impact of AI-based tools and urban big data analytics on city design and planning. It examines how machine learning, computer vision, and data-driven approaches are reshaping urban planning processes, with a focus on how these tools change the roles of designers and planners, what kinds of urban problems they address, and what ethical and governance challenges arise from their adoption. The paper synthesises current applications across smart city contexts and outlines implications for future urban design practice.
