- [[Abstract]]
	- Agent-based modelling has become an important tool to investigate socio-ecological processes, partly driven by increasing demand from decision makers seeking support for understanding potential implications of decisions in complex situations. This paper develops a framework for the parameterisation of human behaviour in agent-based models and identifies twelve distinct sequences for the characterisation and parameterisation of human behaviours. The framework is a first step towards a guide for parameterisation of human behaviour in ABM, with examples provided for the most important sequences.
