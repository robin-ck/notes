- [[Abstract]]
	- Scenarios play a prominent role in policy debates over climate change, but questions continue about how best to use them. This paper describes a new analytic method, based on robust decision making, for suggesting narrative scenarios that emerge naturally from a decision analytic framework. Key scenarios are identified as those most important to the choices facing decision makers and are found through statistical analysis of datasets created by multiple runs of computer simulation models. The resulting scenarios can communicate quantitative judgments about uncertainty and support a well-defined decision process without many drawbacks of current approaches. An application to long-term water planning in California is described.
