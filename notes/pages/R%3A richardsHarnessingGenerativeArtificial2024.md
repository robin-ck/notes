- [[Abstract]]
	- The ongoing biodiversity and climate change crises require society to adopt nature-based solutions that integrate and enhance ecosystems. To achieve successful implementation of nature-based solutions, it is vital to communicate scientific information about their benefits and suitability. This article explores the potential of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) as a tool for automating and scaling up science communication, outreach, and extension for nature-based solutions. Three case study examples are presented: reporting scientific information on ecosystem services for farms, interactively providing guidance on biodiversity-friendly garden design, and visualising potential future landscape scenarios. While GenAI for nature-based solutions offers significant opportunities, this new technology brings risks of bias, false information, data privacy, mistrust, and high energy usage. GenAI offers an opportunity to accelerate the dissemination of nature-based design strategies by synthesising information and producing tailored content for specific users and locations.
