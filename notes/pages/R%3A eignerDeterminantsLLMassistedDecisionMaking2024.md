- [[Abstract]]
	- Decision-making is a fundamental capability in everyday life. Large Language Models (LLMs) provide multifaceted support in enhancing human decision-making processes. This study presents a comprehensive literature analysis providing a structural overview and detailed analysis of determinants impacting decision-making with LLM support. Technological aspects (transparency, prompt engineering), psychological factors (emotions, decision-making styles, trust, mental model), and decision-specific determinants (task difficulty, accountability, information processing) are examined. A dependency framework is developed that systematizes reciprocal interdependencies between these determinants. Key findings identify trust/reliance on LLMs, the user's mental model, and information processing characteristics as significant aspects influencing LLM-assisted decision-making. The findings provide a foundation for improving decision quality in human-AI collaboration and designing more effective LLM interfaces.
