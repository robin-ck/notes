Introduces the NBS-CBS, a hybrid expert system coupling a black-box ANN classifier with a white-box case-based reasoning (CBR) engine over the Urban Nature Atlas (1,100+ cases). The ANN narrows the search space by predicting relevant NbS categories; the CBR retrieves and adapts concrete past cases to provide explainable, inspiration-based recommendations. Tested in an alpha workshop with Eindhoven municipal experts. Explicitly positioned as complementary to the NBS-PSS: CBS handles experience-based solution inspiration, PSS handles spatial site-suitability analysis.
