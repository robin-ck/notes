- [[Abstract]]
	- Conventional urban water management practices aim to meet water supply-demands while conveying wastewater and stormwater away from urban settings. Alternative approaches which consider water demands to be manageable and wastewater and stormwater as valuable resources lack reliable site-specific implementation methodologies. This paper describes the development of the Urban Water Optioneering Tool (UWOT), a decision support tool to facilitate the selection of combinations of water saving strategies and technologies and to support integrated, sustainable water management for new developments. The tool is based on a water balance model that allows investigation of interactions between the major urban water cycle streams, informed by a knowledge library of technological options. Technology selection is driven by a GA algorithm for efficient exploration of the decision space, while quantitative and qualitative sustainability criteria enable comparison between alternative composite water management strategies.
