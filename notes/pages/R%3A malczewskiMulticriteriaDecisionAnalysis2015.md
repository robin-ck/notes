- [[Abstract]]
	- An authoritative and comprehensive reference on multicriteria decision analysis (MCDA) within geographic information science. Covers spatial decision problems, GIS-MCDA integration, criterion weighting methods (including AHP), aggregation procedures, and uncertainty handling. A key reference for research on spatial planning and GIS-based decision support.
