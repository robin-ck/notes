- [[Abstract]]
	- Not in Zotero — add manually. Systematic review of 307 barriers and 301 enablers to NbS implementation, drawn from grey and peer-reviewed literature plus 34 expert workshops. Organised using a PESTEL framework. Core finding: the same factors — stakeholder engagement, evidence base, expertise, and funding — operate as both barriers and enablers depending on context. Polycentric and cross-sectoral governance emerged as the most structurally important enabler.
