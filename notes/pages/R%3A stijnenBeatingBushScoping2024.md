- [[Abstract]]
	- Not in Zotero — add manually. Scoping review of 105 empirical papers on urban NbS trade-offs. Develops a typology of trade-offs along two axes: type (rigid / governance / functional) and planning cycle stage (OF / WITH / ACROSS). Framed through an environmental justice lens (distributional, procedural, recognition). Core argument: trade-offs are inevitable and some are produced by governance practices, so the agenda should shift from overcoming trade-offs to explicitly and accountably dealing with them.
