- [[Abstract]]
	- Not in Zotero — add manually. Introduces SSANTO (Spatial Suitability ANalysis TOol), a GIS-MCDA planning support system for mapping where different WSUD assets are most suitable across a city. Central innovation is a dual suitability framework combining Opportunities (biophysical, socio-economic, planning & governance factors that determine whether a location can support WSUD) and Needs (ecosystem services criteria that determine what a location needs from WSUD). Covers seven WSUD types. Validated against a Melbourne consultant study. Emphasises that suitability is a human concept requiring participatory weight elicitation.
