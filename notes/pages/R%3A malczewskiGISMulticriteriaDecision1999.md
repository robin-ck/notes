- [[Abstract]]
	- A foundational text on integrating geographic information systems (GIS) with multicriteria decision analysis (MCDA). Covers the theoretical basis of spatial decision problems, the role of GIS in structuring and solving MCDA problems, and a range of MCDA methods applicable in spatial contexts. Widely cited as the seminal reference for GIS-MCDA.
